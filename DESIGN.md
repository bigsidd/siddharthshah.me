# siddharthshah.me design system

Adopted 2026-11-14 after a review of every option since the card launched. Short on purpose: it's the rulebook a person or an agent follows to add a page that belongs.

## What the site sells

Judgment: "I build products and the partnerships that make them pay." Every choice is tested against that line. Visual craft supports it; it isn't the point.

## Structure

- **Home (`/`)** is a card: one screen, no scroll, at 375×667, 390×844 and 1280×800. Portrait, time line, name, lede, two short paragraphs, email as text, one links row.
- **Links row:** Projects →, Resume →, then LinkedIn, GitHub and X as icons (each with a screen-reader label). Projects comes first because only this site shows them; the resume is the standard document people look for, so its label stays "Resume".
- **Projects (`/projects/`)** lists every project from `projects.json`. The card never lists projects, so it doesn't change as they grow.
- **Project pages:** a project that lives here gets its own page (`/os/`). A project with its own site links straight out (↗); its story lives there, once.
- **Resume (`/resume/`)** is generated by the resume repo and should take these tokens.

## Brand boundary

- This site owns the tokens below. upwardvx.com uses none of them and appears here only as a plain inline link. Project sites (High Leverage Research, later Midwatch) keep their own brands; none of theirs appears here.
- The card shares Upward Ventures' fonts (Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex), so colour carries the separation. Never use UV's sand #F3EEE4, forest #1E3A2F or terracotta #A8431F, and keep the accent yellow-amber, not red-orange. Avoid High Leverage Research's Newsreader, Public Sans and navy #1F3A5F.

## Tokens (Paper & amber)

| Token | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | #FFFFFF | #111214 |
| `--ink` | #121316 | #EDEDEB |
| `--muted` | #5A5D62 | #A3A5A8 |
| `--accent` | #8A5A00 | #E5A24B |
| `--hair` | ink at 16% | ink at 18% |

Paper base with one accent: the amber of the lights behind the portrait. Contrast on the background: ink 18.6:1 and 16.0:1, muted 6.6:1 and 7.6:1, accent 5.9:1 and 8.6:1. The accent appears in three places only: the time line, link underlines, and arrows. Keep the light background pure white; a warm tint drifts toward UV's sand.

## Type

Bricolage Grotesque 800 for the name (tracking -.03em); IBM Plex Sans 400/600 for the lede and body; IBM Plex Mono 500 uppercase for the time line, links row and small labels.

## Light and dark

Follows the visitor's local time of day: light from 7 AM to 7 PM, dark otherwise, re-checked every minute. A 28px icon button in the card's corner cycles auto (clock) → light (sun) → dark (moon), saved in `localStorage` as `mode` and applied before first paint. The portrait dims slightly in dark mode.

## Time line

"Los Angeles Area" and the live LA time in the accent, then at most one extra in muted text: "replies in the morning" from 10 PM to 7 AM in LA, otherwise "golden hour" in the hour before the Long Beach sunset, otherwise the Long Beach weather in plain words. One extra at a time keeps it to two short rows on a phone. Tapping it shows the visitor's own time and the gap to LA. Never live, travel or phone location.

## Motion

Motion marks an event; if nothing happened, nothing moves. One entrance on load (the card opens and its lines settle, under 600ms), a quiet fade on the time line when the LA minute changes, and a colour fade when the mode is toggled. All of it is skipped with `prefers-reduced-motion`. No idle animation, parallax, cursor effects, or decorative motion.

## Ornament

The portrait keeps its frame: a 1px ink border with a second 1px ink outline 4px outside it. Otherwise none: no tilted pills, stamps or textures. Hairlines sit above and below the links row.

## Checks before shipping a page

- One screen at the three sizes (home only), in light and dark.
- `python3 .github/scripts/check_site.py` passes.
- Reads fully without JS.
- No employer named outside `/resume/`.
