# siddharthshah.me — CLAUDE.md

Personal card, bigsidd identity. Static pages in `public/`, deployed to Render (not Vercel) via `render.yaml`. The look and its rules are in `DESIGN.md`.

- Keep it a card: one screen with no scroll at 375×667, 390×844 and 1280×800. Name, a one-line lede, two short paragraphs of proof (what I do, then how I work and Upward Ventures), the hello@ address as text, then one links row: Projects →, Resume →, and LinkedIn, GitHub and X as icons. No selling; Upward Ventures gets one plain inline link.
- Projects live on `/projects/`, generated from `projects.json` by `scripts/build-projects.py` (don't hand-edit the page). Adding a project is one entry plus that command; the card never changes as projects grow. A project with its own site links straight out (↗); `/os/` lives here. A case study lives at `/projects/<slug>/`, generated from `case-studies/<slug>/page.json` and its screenshots by `scripts/build-case-studies.py` (the field list is in its docstring; 300 words at most; the project's `projects.json` href must point at it). Every page has the same shape: a head inside the first screen at 390 and 1280px (headline, one-sentence summary, a three-fact strip, the outcome, the hero image), then the story beside "The calls", then a `pair` or `rail` of fixed-ratio images. Keep it to 2 screens at 1280px and 3 at 390px. Work projects set `at_work`, which links to `/resume/` and never names the employer.
- Never name the current employer on the card. `/resume/` is the exception: it is generated by `~/code/bigsidd/resume/build.sh` from `resume.md` (don't hand-edit it), carries noindex (meta tag and `X-Robots-Tag` in `render.yaml`), and the card links to it (Sidd's call, 2026-11-07: a hosted resume signals nothing on its own).
- Its own look, kept apart from upwardvx.com and from High Leverage Research: Bricolage Grotesque and IBM Plex (shared with UV, so colour does the separating), Paper & amber in a light and a dark mode, and the framed portrait. It follows the visitor's local time of day (light 7 AM–7 PM); a small toggle cycles auto → light → dark and is remembered. Motion: one entrance on load, a quiet fade on the time line when the LA minute changes, and the portrait dims slightly in dark mode. The time line reads "Los Angeles Area" + live LA time (tap for your own time and the gap to LA), then at most one extra: "replies in the morning" late at night (10 PM–7 AM LA), else "golden hour" in the hour before sunset, else the Long Beach weather from our own Worker (`worker/weather.js`) as plain words. Never live, travel or phone location (Sidd reconfirmed, 2026-11-14). The favicon is an LA-hour clock (day / golden / night) in the card's colours, redrawn on the minute. No scroll, cursor or other idle effects. Small progressive JS only; the card must read fully without it.
- hello@siddharthshah.me forwards to iCloud and Gmail via Cloudflare Email Routing and the `hello-forward` Email Worker (`worker/hello-forward.js`).
- No location finer than Long Beach city level, and never live or travel location.
- Verify in a real browser at 375px, 390px and 1280px, in light and dark, before calling a change done; run `python3 .github/scripts/check_site.py`.
